ABOUT THE LIBRARY

About Taiyaku Bunko

A free library for reading public-domain classics in their original languages alongside newly prepared Japanese translations.

Texts and sources

We select works whose original texts are in the public domain in Japan and the United States. Each work links to its source. The interface is available in Japanese and English; switching the interface does not translate a work into English. English-language works include their English originals.

Translation and editorial approach

Japanese translations are newly prepared with AI assistance. We compare them with the original for meaning, context, narrative voice and character relationships. Some translations remain works in progress. We do not claim that the use of AI guarantees accuracy or that a professional human translator has certified every text.

New additions focus on prose: fiction, essays and criticism. Earlier poetry remains available. New work pages include reading introductions and translation notes. For scholarly citation, consult the source edition as well.

Reading

Choose Parallel text, Japanese, or the original language. Text size, reading view and bookmarks are stored in your browser. They do not sync between devices. Read without registration or payment.

Corrections and contact

The Taiyaku Bunko editorial project maintains this library. Report a translation error, missing passage or source issue through the project’s issue tracker, giving the title and relevant passage. A GitHub account may be required to submit an issue.
