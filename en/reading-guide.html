READING GUIDE

Reading parallel texts

Follow a story, study Japanese, or compare how two languages shape the same scene.

Choose a starting point

Try The Kiss for conversational strategy, or Ripe Figs for a brief scene. A Pair of Silk Stockings builds a longer story through concrete details. Short does not always mean easy: irony and omissions can make a brief text demanding.

Read one paragraph at a time

Select the original-language view and read a paragraph for its situation and speaker. Return to Parallel text to compare the Japanese. Then read the original again. For Japanese learners, begin with the Japanese column and use the original to check meaning.

Compare meaning, not word order

Translations may rearrange clauses or leave out repeated subjects to sound natural in Japanese. Look closely at negation, imagery, politeness and shifts in voice. Parallel text is not a word-by-word glossary.

Save your place

Text size, reading view and bookmarks are stored in the same browser. Use Resume reading when offered. Preferences do not sync across devices and may disappear when browser data is cleared or private browsing ends.

Sources and notes

Each work links to its source; new additions include reading introductions and translation notes. Literary texts stay in their stated languages when you switch the interface. There is no automatic English translation of French, German or Russian works. Consult the source edition for scholarly quotation.
